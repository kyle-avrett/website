Guidebook Studio helps healthcare teams turn SOPs, policies, checklists,
and training docs into searchable field guides. Answers stay grounded in
approved sources instead of guessy chat output.

I built the platform with RAG workflows, an MCP service layer, and
guardrails for scoped retrieval, source citation, and unsupported
clinical or operational claims.

</OverviewCard>

## What it does

- Ingests operating procedures and company knowledge into searchable guides with source-backed answers.
- Exposes approved knowledge sources through MCP so AI agents can retrieve current SOPs, policies, checklists, and training docs.
- Limits unsupported claims with healthcare-focused guardrails, scoped retrieval, and source citations.
- Runs in production with paying customers.
